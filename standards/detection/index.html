Production detection standard

How I maintain production detections.

My operating model for taking a detection from a behavior hypothesis into production, then validating, monitoring, changing, and eventually retiring it.

01 · Lifecycle

Eight gates from intent to retirement.

The sequence follows how a detection is operated. Logic quality is only one part of a control that also depends on telemetry, analyst usability, health, regression testing, and change discipline.

01

Intent

Define the attacker behavior or security condition, why it matters, and what decision the control is expected to enable.

EXIT: behavior is specific enough to test.
02

Telemetry contract

Define required sources, fields, timestamps, normalization, expected latency, and how missing or degraded data will be detected.

EXIT: absence cannot masquerade as safety.
03

Logic

Implement the smallest defensible query or correlation path, preserve useful source context, and make aggregation semantics explicit.

EXIT: behavior maps to inspectable evidence.
04

Validation

Replay known-positive scenarios, test benign and boundary cases, and verify the logic behaves as expected with incomplete or late data. Record per-rule precision from the first day, not after cutover.

EXIT: expected and non-expected paths are exercised.
05

Analyst contract

Explain why the detection fired, what evidence matters, what uncertainty remains, and what the next investigation step should be.

EXIT: an analyst can act without reverse-engineering the search.
06

Operating health

Monitor source health, execution failures, runtime, volume, precision, and abnormal silence independently from detection output.

EXIT: control degradation is observable.
07

Change control

Tune with evidence, rerun regression cases, document the reason for change, and preserve a rollback path when the production effect is uncertain.

EXIT: changes are reproducible and reviewable.
08

Retirement

Retire or merge a control when telemetry disappears, the behavior is covered better elsewhere, or analyst cost exceeds retained security value.

EXIT: stale rules do not become permanent inventory.
02 · Release gate

Before a detection reaches production.

Not every environment can automate every check, but I want these questions answered before a rule starts interrupting analysts.

DATA

Telemetry is trustworthy

  • Required fields exist and mean what the detection assumes.
  • Timestamp and latency behavior fit the search window.
  • Feed health is monitored separately.
LOGIC

Behavior is explainable

  • Conditions and aggregation have a clear security rationale.
  • Source context survives normalization where it matters.
  • Thresholds are treated as tunable assumptions, not facts.
TEST

Expected paths were exercised

  • Known-positive replay or representative fixture.
  • Benign / false-positive case.
  • Boundary and missing-data case.
OPS

The control can be operated

  • Analyst output explains why it fired.
  • Execution and silence can be monitored.
  • Owner, review path, and rollback are clear.
